Which habit costs less

Same three months, same daily consumption, same opening balance — only the recharge timing differs. Low‑balance tops up whenever the balance dips below the threshold; monthly tops up on the 1st. Cost is what the meter consumes (energy + VAT + the fixed charges that actually fire), not what is deposited.

Because consumption and the slab counter are identical, energy and VAT are identical to the paisa. The only lever is how many months trigger a first‑recharge fixed charge R‑16. Rows below are clickable; totals track the tariff above.
